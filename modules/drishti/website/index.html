The Problem

Existing tools weren't built for India.

Apps like Seeing AI and Be My Eyes work only in English. India has the world's largest blind population — and almost no accessibility technology in their language.

🌍
Language barrier
Global assistive apps speak English. Over 90% of India's visually impaired population primarily speaks regional languages.
💸
Prohibitive cost
Most premium assistive devices cost ₹20,000–₹80,000. Smartphones are already in everyone's pocket.
🧭
No independence
Simple tasks — reading a label, crossing a road, knowing who's in the room — require constant help from others.

Real Situations

The moments that matter.

🛒
Shopping alone
Point at a product on a shelf. Drishti reads the label, tells you what it is, and compares prices across platforms so you can shop independently.
"What does this packet say?" → "Parle-G biscuits, 200g, ₹20"
🚶
Walking safely
Navigation mode scans for obstacles in real-time and gives audio alerts before you encounter steps, barriers, or oncoming people.
"Stop — there are two steps directly ahead"
💊
Reading medicine labels
Point at a medicine box or prescription. Drishti reads the drug name, dosage, and instructions aloud — helping avoid dangerous mistakes.
"Paracetamol 500mg — take 1 tablet after meals"
📚
Studying & reading
Scan any notebook, textbook, or printed document. Drishti reads handwritten notes and printed text aloud — perfect for students.
"Newton's second law: F = ma, force equals mass times acceleration"
